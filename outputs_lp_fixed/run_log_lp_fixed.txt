============================================================================================================
lp_v5_fixed.py - harvest plan for one 9-batch plant campaign (mixed-integer linear programme, review fixes 1 to 7)
============================================================================================================
The model in three sentences:
  Objective: first make the lowest forecast harvest titre among the campaign's 9 batches as high as
    possible, then, holding that floor, make the total titre as large as possible, and then use as few
    interventions, as little temperature shift, as little extra feed and as few inoculation slips as
    possible.
  Decisions: on culture day 7, which few batches get an intervention, how much extra feed (0 to 100 L) and
    how far to lower the temperature (0 to 3 C) they get, a provisional harvest culture day (13, 14 or 15)
    with an inoculation slip of 0 or 1 day, and whether a batch must fall back to an early harvest; on
    culture day 13 a rolling rule fixes each batch's final harvest day from its measured viability, titre
    and dead-cell density.
  Constraints: an intervention budget per campaign and at most one intervention per week, viability at
    harvest at or above the trigger unless the batch falls back to day 13, working volume, osmolality,
    Protein A capacity, dead-cell density and downstream time per harvest, tank turnaround with one
    maintenance day per tank, at least K days between harvests unless pooled, no Sunday harvests, one
    blocked downstream day and, where it applies, a site shutdown.
Solver: HiGHS (highspy) through PuLP 4.1.0, MIP gap 1e-9, feasibility tolerances 1e-9
Emergency harvests on a Sunday: EMERGENCY_OVERRIDES_SUNDAY = True (ASSUMPTION: call-out crew)
Settled base case: FEED_TITRE_EFFECT = 0.0 (ASSUMPTION), TEMP_SHIFT_ALLOWED = False (DATA); scenario 'bench trial confirms levers' = {'FEED_TITRE_EFFECT': 0.5, 'TEMP_SHIFT_ALLOWED': True, 'TEMP_EFF': 2.0, 'TEMP_VIAB': 2.0, 'MAX_INTERVENTIONS_PER_CAMPAIGN': 2}

[11] Limitations
  1. Settled base case: FEED_TITRE_EFFECT = 0.0 and TEMP_SHIFT_ALLOWED = False, so interventions add nothing
     and the base plan is a harvest-day plan. Every lever result comes from the 'bench trial confirms
     levers' scenario, whose effects are assumptions the AZ data cannot test.
  2. The temperature evidence is 4 shifted against 23 unshifted bench runs from different campaigns: -40% by
     median day-14 titre, -33% by mean; not a controlled comparison.
  3. Downstream fences are proxies. Dead-cell density (from VCD and the VCD/TCD viability index) stands in
     for clarification load and HCP/DNA release; monomer % is measured only in the Endpoint Set (one value
     per harvest, all cell lines and scales). HCP, DNA and glycans are not measured anywhere in the data, so
     no product-quality limit can be checked directly.
  4. Annual views assume every batch of the year matches this 9-run campaign's kg per batch, on fixed 17- or
     18-day tank cycles.
  5. EMERGENCY_OVERRIDES_SUNDAY = True (ASSUMPTION: call-out crew): a day-13 emergency harvest may fall on a
     Sunday (C1 does). With False it waits for the next working day in the window.
  6. The day-13 rule projects viability with bench median changes (-4.18 to day 14, a further -4.94 to day
     15); they were too cautious for C37 and cannot foresee a collapse like C1's.
  7. Nine plant runs of 50 to 500 L stand in for 2,000 L batches, rescaled so business as usual averages 8.0
     g/L; C25 alone drives the Protein A and viability limits.
  8. Day 16 has titre data for 5 of the 9 runs, so the '+1 day harvest window' gain is forecast only.
  9. Perfect foresight maximises the lowest batch and the total, not the spread, so it is no benchmark for
     SD; spread is reported as SD_change_vs_BAU (plan SD - BAU SD) rather than as a share of perfect
     foresight.
  10. The budget, calendar, maintenance days, spacing and Protein A margin are assumptions (see the CONFIG
     labels).
