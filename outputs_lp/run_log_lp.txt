lp_v5.py - harvest plan for one 9-batch plant campaign (mixed-integer linear programme)
========================================================================================================
The model in three sentences:
  Objective: first make the lowest forecast harvest titre among the campaign's 9 batches as high as
    possible, then, holding that floor, make the campaign's total titre as large as possible.
  Decisions: for each batch, the harvest culture day (13, 14 or 15) with an inoculation slip of 0 or 1
    day, whether to intervene, how much extra feed to add (0 to 100 L) and how far to lower the
    temperature (0 to 3 C), and, in one scenario, whether to pool its harvest with the next batch.
  Constraints: viability at harvest at or above the trigger, working volume, osmolality, at most one
    intervention per calendar week, Protein A capacity per harvest, tank turnaround with one maintenance
    day per tank, at least K days between harvests unless pooled, no Sunday harvests, one blocked
    downstream day and, where it applies, a site shutdown.
Solver: HiGHS (highspy) through PuLP 4.1.0, MIP gap 1e-9
